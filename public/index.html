✦ How it works

Under the hood.

Dezignmail is powered entirely by Cloudflare's edge network — no traditional servers, no databases to maintain, no single point of failure.

Architecture

When you send an email to any address on our domains, here's what happens:

  • Cloudflare's Email Routing receives the SMTP message via our MX records
  • Our Email Worker parses the raw message using postal-mime
  • Email metadata (from, subject, body) is stored in Cloudflare D1 (SQLite)
  • Attachments are stored in Cloudflare R2 object storage
  • Your browser polls the API every 5 seconds to show new messages

Email validation records

All our domains are configured with:

  • MX records — Cloudflare's route1/2/3.mx.cloudflare.net servers receive your mail
  • SPF — Authorizes Cloudflare to send on behalf of our domains
  • DKIM — Cryptographic signature to prevent spoofing
  • DMARC — Policy enforcement for both SPF and DKIM

Privacy by architecture

Your inbox address is stored only in your browser's session storage. If you close or refresh the tab, the session ends. No server-side sessions, no cookies, no user accounts.

Data retention

All emails are automatically deleted after 3 hours. Manual deletion wipes everything immediately. Attachments stored in R2 are deleted alongside the email record. We retain nothing.

Powered by

  • Cloudflare Pages — Frontend hosting & API
  • Cloudflare Email Routing — SMTP reception
  • Cloudflare D1 — Email metadata storage
  • Cloudflare R2 — Attachment storage
  • Hono — TypeScript web framework

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Dezignwise

Overview

Dezignmail is operated by Dezignwise. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. The short version: we collect almost nothing, retain nothing long-term, and share nothing.

Data we collect

When you use Dezignmail, the following data is temporarily processed:

  • Email content — When an email is delivered to your temporary address, the sender, subject, body, and attachments are stored in Cloudflare D1 and R2 to display in your inbox.
  • Inbox address — Stored only in your browser's session storage. Never sent to our servers as an identifier.
  • Server logs — Cloudflare may collect standard request logs (IP, User-Agent, timestamp) as part of their infrastructure operations. We do not access or process these logs.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Account information (we have no accounts)
  • Cookies or tracking identifiers
  • Analytics or behavioral data
  • Payment information
  • Your real email address

Data retention

All emails and attachments are automatically deleted after 3 hours. If you manually delete your inbox, all associated data is deleted immediately and permanently. We have no backups of individual email content.

Third-party services

Dezignmail is built on Cloudflare's infrastructure (Pages, Email Routing, D1, R2). Cloudflare's own privacy policy applies to data processed through their network. We use no other third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties. Period.

Your rights

Since we don't collect personally identifiable information and all data is ephemeral, standard data rights (access, deletion, portability) are satisfied by design. If you have concerns, contact us at privacy@dezignwise.eu.cc.

Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
