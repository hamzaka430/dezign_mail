  Dezignmail DNS Records for dezignwise.online
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Replace YOUR_VPS_IP with your DigitalOcean Droplet's IP.

── Required Records ──────────────────────────────────

Type    Name                     Value
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MX      @                        10 mail.dezignwise.online
A       mail                     YOUR_VPS_IP
TXT     @                        v=spf1 mx ip4:YOUR_VPS_IP -all
TXT     _dmarc                   v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:postmaster@dezignwise.online

── DKIM Record (generated by setup-postfix.sh) ───────

Type    Name                     Value
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TXT     mail._domainkey          v=DKIM1; h=sha256; k=rsa; p=<PUBLIC_KEY_FROM_SETUP>

(The public key is printed at the end of setup-postfix.sh output)

── Verification Commands ─────────────────────────────

# Check MX records
dig MX dezignwise.online

# Check SPF
dig TXT dezignwise.online

# Check DKIM
dig TXT mail._domainkey.dezignwise.online

# Check DMARC
dig TXT _dmarc.dezignwise.online

# Test email delivery (from VPS)
echo "Hello from Dezignmail" | mail -s "Test" swift.fox.1234@dezignwise.online

# Check mail logs
tail -f /var/log/postfix/mail.log

# Verify DKIM signing
opendkim-testkey -d dezignwise.online -s mail -vvv
